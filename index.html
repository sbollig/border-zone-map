Where do Americans live relative to the border zone?

54.1%of U.S. residents, 184.1M people, live inside the 100-mile zone (2024 county estimates, statutory reading)

Key Takeaways

  • 117.6M people live within 25 miles of a boundary, the band where officers may also enter private land.
  • The zone covers 54.1% of the population under the statute, rising to 60.5% if CBP's Great Lakes reading holds.
  • 156.0M people, 45.9%, live outside the zone under the statutory reading.

About the data · sources · accuracy

Not legal advice. This map illustrates a legal boundary as described in federal statute and regulation. It is not a government map and does not describe your rights in any specific situation.

Legal basis. 8 U.S.C. § 1357(a)(3) (warrantless search within a "reasonable distance" of an external boundary; 25 miles for private land) and 8 CFR 287.1(a) ("reasonable distance" = 100 air miles; "external boundary" = land boundaries and the territorial sea). Source: Cornell Legal Information Institute, retrieved 2026-09-14.

The Great Lakes claim is CBP's asserted position as described by the ACLU of Michigan, The Border's Long Shadow (March 2021). That report cites no CBP document for the claim, and no federal court opinion deciding the question was found as of 2026-09-14. It is drawn separately so the two readings are not confused.

Geometry. Country, state, and lake outlines from Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain) via world-atlas. Distances are geodesic air miles computed in the browser with Turf.js. Expect errors of a few miles near complex coastlines and small islands.

Population. U.S. Census Bureau, county population estimates, vintage 2024 (July 1, 2024), 3,144 counties, 340.1M total. A county is counted in a band if its Census internal-point centroid falls there, so large counties straddling a band edge are all-in or all-out. Treat figures as estimates, not parcel-level counts.

Cities. Natural Earth populated places, filtered to ≥ 300,000 population. Only cities inside a zone are labeled; when labels would overlap, smaller cities show as dots until you zoom in.

Software. Leaflet (BSD-2), Turf.js (MIT), topojson-client (ISC). No tracking, cookies, or analytics on this page.
